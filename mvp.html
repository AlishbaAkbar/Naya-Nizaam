Why Kasur?

Naya Nizaam is designed around a local maintenance problem rather than claiming to solve city-wide flooding.

• NDMA’s 2025 Post-Monsoon Review included Kasur among districts discussed in relation to poor access to clean water and sanitation in some temporary shelters.
• Peer-reviewed research has documented wastewater, drainage and groundwater-contamination challenges in Kasur, particularly around tannery-related industrial activity.
Hotspots, household counts, worker profiles, and scores shown in this prototype are illustrative demonstration data.

Help & Support

Quick answers about using the NAYA NAZAM prototype.

How do I submit a report?

Open Report, add the requested details, and submit. Your report is illustrative prototype data.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is optional and can help attach your current position to a report. Your browser asks permission before sharing it.

Are map hotspots officially verified?

No. Map hotspots are illustrative prototype markers and are not officially verified flood hotspots.

How do I add a photo?

Choose the photo option and allow camera access when prompted, or select an image from your device.
